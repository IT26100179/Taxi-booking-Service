Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, driving, and fares on Gamana.

Simply click "Book a Ride Now" or register an account. Enter your pickup address and destination, choose your vehicle category (Bike, Tuk, Car, or Van), and confirm your request. Nearby drivers will receive your offer instantly.

Fares are calculated transparently using an official formula: Total = Base Fare + (Distance in KM × Per KM Rate) - Promo Discount. We provide upfront pricing before you confirm the ride.

Click on "Register as Driver", provide your name, phone number, vehicle type (Bike, Tuk, Car, Van), and vehicle license plate number. Once registered, our admin team reviews and approves your account so you can begin accepting rides immediately.

Active promo codes (such as WELCOME50) can be entered during booking to deduct a fixed amount or percentage from your ride fare.
